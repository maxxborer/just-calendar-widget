Native widgets

Built with SwiftUI and WidgetKit. Add a layout from the macOS widget gallery.

No calendar access

No accounts, analytics, or access to your events. The app links to GitHub when you check for an update.

Your week, your way

Month names, the first weekday, and time zone follow your Mac. Warm surfaces and a blue accent keep dates clear.

On your desktop in a minute.

  1. Download the latest DMG and move the app to Applications.
  2. Open Just Calendar Widget from Applications once.
  3. Control-click your desktop, choose Edit Widgets, and search for Just Calendar Widget.

Preview builds use an ad-hoc signature. See the installation guide if macOS blocks opening.
